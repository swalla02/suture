# Language gaps the airline policy hit

What `suture/airline.st` could not say directly, and how it says it instead. Version 1 (Stage 1, tag `rules-airline-frozen`) hit eleven gaps. Slice 64 (spec appendix BO) closed the worst, and version 2 of `airline.st` is rewritten with its forms. The status column says where each gap stands now.

| # | Gap | Workaround in v1 | Cost of the workaround | Now (v2, slice 64) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A formula cannot compare or branch (`fee = 30 if weight > 50`). | Every fee is a row of a table, and a pool (`uses … where`) picks the row whose bounds hold. | The overweight and oversize tables become data (`charges.csv`), not sentences. | **Closed.** `0 USD if its weight is at most 50, otherwise 30 USD if …`; the tables are formulas on Region and Bag. |
| 2 | No `max`. | A bag matches one size row and one weight row; `prefer Charge: highest amount` takes the higher. | Each table needs a $0 row, or a bag within the limits gets no row. | **Closed.** `the larger of its oversize and its overweight`. |
| 3 | A sentence cannot read what a part was granted; `taking` cannot read the candidate. | The harness adds up the amounts of the granted rows. | The total is not a Suture value: no rule can bound it, and `suture explain` does not show it. | **Narrowed.** Each bag's fee is a Suture value, `define: a Bag's fee is its Base's Rate's amount + …`, which rules and scenarios read. The trip's total of its bags' fees is still a sum the harness makes: a running total of a defined value across units is not in the language. |
| 4 | A `define` cannot be read through a reference (`its trip is far`), on a list element, or on a candidate inside a `where`. | The conditions are written out in full. | The Waiver's condition is seven lines, and the policy's "complimentary" is not one named state. | **Worked around better.** The Waiver's condition is two lines, since what it needs is now stored formulas (`free` on the Trip, `saving` on the Bag), which a candidate carries. A defined value is still read only in its own unit. |
| 5 | A taken total (`the total waivers taken from …`) is known only in its own unit. | The overweight pick sits on the Bag, behind a `weigh` event that the harness sends after the Allowances. | The order of inputs carries meaning: weighed too early, a bag pays the 50 lbs allowance. | **Closed.** The fee is a defined value, so it follows `it is waived` whenever that changes. There is no `weigh` event. |
| 6 | Only one kind may rank a pool kind with `prefer`. | Two row kinds, Charges and Graces, for paid and free allowances; the size rows appear in both. | Duplicated rows, which can drift apart. | **Gone.** No rows are picked for these fees. |
| 7 | A unit holds at most 4096 bytes, and a granted row is carried with everything it refers to. | One row per region, not a set of regions; each bag picks its own fee row; one Allowance per free place; the Waiver's pool leaves out bags that save nothing. | Four changes of shape for size alone, each found by a fault at run time, not by the compiler. | **Eased.** A unit holds 64 KiB. The compiler still bounds no size, so a large enough pool would still fault at run time. |
| 8 | A pool cannot rank by a computed difference (what the allowance saves). | Three hand-derived tiers: heavy and snug, heavy and close, plump and snug. | The tiers hold for these tables only; a new fee could reorder them, and nothing would say so. | **Closed.** `saving: Money = its charge - its grace` and `prefer Bag: highest saving`. |
| 9 | A pool gathers 8 candidates by default before ranking. | `candidates 16`. | With 8, a 10-bag case could silently miss its best bag. | Open; v2 keeps `candidates 16`. |
| 10 | The policy text says "Not accepted" where RuleArena's script charges the highest fee. | `charges.csv` follows the script, the ground truth. Suture could say "not accepted" (no row, so the part waits), but then S1 and R would differ on bags the generator never makes. | A refusal the text asks for is not modelled. | Unchanged: the formulas follow the script. |
| 11 | Signed numbers, shares and margins (from the spec's known list). | Not needed here. | — | — |

Version 2 has 7 kinds instead of 11, no fee rows beyond the policy's own checked-bag table, no event, and no ranking worked out by hand. It gives the same answer as version 1 on every dev and test case, with true and with extracted facts, and equals R on the 400 random trips.

What remains for tax: item 3's sum across units of a defined value, item 7's size bound, and item 9.
